### The guardrails review (fixes made)

No golden rule was broken. What it found, and what changed:

- **Busy days:** the "places still open" alert looked only at the 50 soonest sessions, so on a big
  day sessions after those were never reached. It now works through every session, 50 at a time.
- **The summary's figures** came from the page's list, which stops at 100. They're now counted on
  their own, from 08:00 yesterday (not "the last 24 hours", which was wrong when it went late).
- **Two alert jobs at once** could lock two people in opposite orders (Postgres would undo one and
  retry). Each batch now claims people in one order.
- **A subscription follows only the person who chose it.** Opening Updates, or the browser renewing
  a subscription, now changes only a subscription already that office user's. A casual's in the
  same browser is never taken on by itself.
- **Smaller fixes:** the log says "Sending … a notification" (it's written before it goes). A sixth
  browser dropping the oldest is logged. A taken-over browser counts as its new owner's newest. A
  request body is never read past 4 KB, even when its size isn't declared. "1 needs action".
- **Also found by the full test run:** the firm data export now leaves out office browsers' push
  keys, as it does casuals'.
- **New tests:** a disabled admin gets nothing, more sessions than one batch, two jobs at once, the
  summary's window, renewing, taking over, and the request body checks.

### The guardrails review of 26.5 (fixes made)

No golden rule was broken. What it found, and what changed:

- **"Add shifts" could join any event.** That included a planner's (the planner would then get
  bookings on a session it never sent), a site and day's automatic one, or one at a firm whose rota
  is a Google Sheet. It now joins only events the office named itself, and the button shows only
  then.
- **"Offers are going out" was read from the urgent flag alone.** Offers stop when a session fills,
  runs out of people or is hidden, while it stays urgent. The page now says so only while they
  really are going out. "Offer to the best 5" starts stopped offers again (logged as such).
- **One "Yes" could offer a whole event days ahead.** Urgent offers skip a casual's 3-a-day limit,
  so a small pool could get many messages at once. "Offer N open places" now covers only sessions
  inside the firm's urgent window (24 hours by default); further ahead, each place is offered from
  its card. This is recorded as decision 10 for Dylan to confirm or change.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - each session is offered in one locked step (no count of a session filled meanwhile);
  - the button's number matches what it offers;
  - answers are heard after the page redraws, and focus goes into the "Yes" step and back;
  - names show in full (cut to fit);
  - a started or filled session's empty places show as "Not open";
  - "why it's open" names only the cancellation whose place is still open.
- **New tests:** restarting stopped offers, nothing to do while they go out, the window, "Add
  shifts" refused for a planner's, an automatic and a sheet firm's event.

### The guardrails review of 26.6 (fixes made)

No golden rule was broken. What it found, and what changed:

- **High: Edit could leave asks open.** Moving a session with nobody booked through Edit, or
  through the planner's change, left asks open. The office could then book someone for a time they
  never asked for. Now every move closes the asks still waiting. A "Not this time" closes too,
  since it was for the old session, so they may ask for the new one.
- **"Book the best N" chose again on the server.** It could book someone the office hadn't
  confirmed. Now it books only the people named in the "Yes", and only while each one's ask is
  still waiting. That check runs inside the booking's own transaction, under the shift's lock.
  Anyone answered since is reported as "Not booked, their ask was answered since".
- **"Not this time" could reach someone already booked, or who had asked again.** The message is
  now skipped unless the answer still stands and they hold no place, however they were booked
  (the sheet's and a connector's bookings too).
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - the casual's words now promise only what happens ("we'll message you when they decide");
  - the list counts places as booking does;
  - the list ranks everyone who could take it before cutting to 100;
  - the "already sent" check is narrowed to the session.
- **New tests:** a move by Edit and by the office's move, "Book the best N" with an answered ask, a
  refusal and a full session, the list's tabs, order and filter, and the message skipped or held
  for quiet hours.
- **Left for later** (Parking lot): sheet and file imports that retime a session don't close asks,
  and don't expire offers either; asks left unanswered at the start; taking back an ask.
