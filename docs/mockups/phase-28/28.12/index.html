Help and the cancel sheet, before and after

What a casual sees on Help and in "Can't make it?", now and after 28.12. Phones at their real 390px, light and dark; the laptop row is 1,280px wide at 150% scaling (an 853px window). Made-up firm and casual: Acme Events (fair rotation, its seed settings), Robin Ashdown. A dashed blue outline marks what 28.12 adds or changes.

  • Help gets "The firm's rules for taking shifts": each line from the very parser the rules use, worded per allocation mode. A setting that doesn't parse leaves its line out. No line says an offer runs out: offers close only when the shift fills, moves or is cancelled.
  • Help gets "Why can't I see a shift?": role, qualifications, travel area (only with travel areas on), days off, rest gap, weekly cap, sessions not opened to casuals, and fair rotation's "You can take it from …".
  • Help names the buttons she really sees ("Your travel area", then "Set your travel area (or Change your travel area)"; "tap the shift, then Ask for a change"; "Send this photo"), and every "Contact the office" is a "Message the office" link.
  • The cancel sheet: the warning and the result are slips, the reasons choice cards, 12px top corners. The footer names the button. "Tell us what happened" only after a recorded strike. A started shift links "Message the office" about it.

Help: the firm's rules, and why a shift may not show

Before, "Taking a shift" listed reasons in lower case and said "Some shifts go first to people with fewer hours that week" to every firm, first come or not. After, two topics with the firm's own numbers.

Help: no dead ends, and buttons as they read

Struck-through words go; the new ones carry the dashed outline. Each "Message the office" is a link to Messages, 44px to tap; about pay it opens Messages about pay (a person, 28.10).

"Can't make it?": the warning, the reasons and the footer

The warning's words and their stored version (v2) are unchanged; only its box becomes a slip. The reasons become choice cards (only the chosen one has the ink edge).

Done, and a shift that has already started

Before, "If something stopped you, tell us what happened." followed "No strike.", and a started shift said "Contact the office." with no way to do it. After, the prompt follows a recorded strike only, and a started shift has "Message the office" (→ /messages?shift={id}&about=started, 28.2's link).
