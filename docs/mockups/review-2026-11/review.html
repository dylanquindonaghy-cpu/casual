The short version. The office looks finished and the casual app works well. The biggest problems are in the words and in what the screens don't let you do: "Who worked?" makes you open sessions by number, "Need updating" has no button to chase anyone, cancellations hide the 48-hour cases in small grey text, and WhatsApp still appears in six places. The data you want for reliability already exists (no-shows, late cancellations and strikes are all logged); nothing adds it up per person yet.

Fix first

Things that are wrong, contradict each other or would confuse someone you show the demo to. Most are small.

  1. HighThe demo's casual login number opens an empty account

    The runbook says to try the casual app with 07700 900101 ("Zoe"). That number belongs to one of the five casuals the seed keeps free of shifts, so the app says "No shifts booked" everywhere. The story's Zoe Adeyemi, with a booking tomorrow, a CSAS shift to take and a photo to renew, is 07700 900400.

    Fix: point the runbook (step 10) at 07700 900400. Done in this branch: it only changes the instructions.

  2. HighEvery demo history row says it happened at 04:00 today

    The notice report lists "Booked 04:00 on Mon 2 Nov" for a shift on Sun 18 Oct "with 6 days' notice", and every shift's History says "04:00 on Mon 2 Nov". The action log can't be backdated: each row is stamped when it's written, and the demo is reloaded at 04:00 each night. So bookings appear to be made after the shift they're for.

    Fix: let the demo loader (only) write each history row with the story's own time. Real use is not affected.

  3. HighWhatsApp is still on six screens

    Offer list: "by WhatsApp". Casual Profile: "Send shift messages by WhatsApp / Text message" (WhatsApp is the default for everyone). Profile: "You'll get messages by WhatsApp or text". Pay dashboard: "Your shifts line … (WhatsApp, text and calls)". Automatic replies in the app: "Send OFFICE to reach a person" (a WhatsApp keyword shown inside the app). Pilot metrics: "WhatsApp messages per day" (fine there: it counts the old way).

    Fix: change H below. Most of it is the planned Phase 15.1 ("Texts only").

  4. MediumThe Pay dashboard shows two different pay days

    Under the title: "Pay Fri 13 Nov". In the cards: "Next pay date Fri 6 Nov". Both are true (6 Nov pays earlier work from the imported pay records, 13 Nov pays this week's shifts) but the page doesn't say which work each pays for.

    Fix: "Next pay day: Fri 6 Nov, for shifts in the week of 26 Oct" and "This week's shifts are paid Fri 13 Nov".

  5. MediumA cancellations card measures two things at once

    The card is titled "Under 48 hours' notice this week" but its number is "0 strikes issued". A short-notice cancellation by the office gives no strike, so the two numbers differ.

    Fix: three cards: cancellations, under 48 hours, strikes issued (change F).

  6. MediumCasuals are told they "apply" but they book instantly

    Home and Open shifts say "shifts you can apply for"; the button on the shift says "Take shift" and books it at once (first yes wins). "Apply" makes people wait for an answer that never comes.

    Fix: say "take" everywhere, unless you choose the apply-and-pick option in change G.

  7. Medium"Needs updating" ignores missing staff photos

    Most casuals show "Staff photo: Missing" in one column and "Needs updating: Nothing" in the next.

    Fix: count a missing or due photo as needing updating, or stop showing it as a problem.

  8. LowThe phone dashboard cuts off a tab, and the chart labels crowd

    At 390px, "Next week" is pushed off the tab row and the 14 day numbers under the chart touch each other.

    Fix: on a phone, show this week's 7 days and a "Next week" switch; let the tab row scroll with a fade.

  9. LowPilot metrics are always empty on the demo

    The page says "No daily figures for these dates yet" and "Portal adoption 0% (0 of 5)". The figures are worked out each night at 00:30 and the demo is reloaded at 04:00, which looks like why they never build up (worth confirming).

    Fix: have the demo loader work the figures out after each reload, and say "Last worked out at 00:30" next to them.

Your decisions

I need these before building. My recommendation is in green.

1. What is a "contract"?

The app knows events, sites (venues) and roles, but has no idea of a client or contract. A resourcer who "looks after the Arena and the council patrols" could mean sites, or the client who pays for several sites.

Recommended: a client (contract) that owns one or more sites, set by the admin, so a resourcer picks clients and sees every site under them. It's a new table, so it needs your OK (schema).

2. "Who has applied": keep instant booking, or let casuals apply?

Today a casual taps "Take shift" and it's theirs at once. Nobody applies, so there's no list of applicants to show.

Recommended: a setting per event: "Casuals take places" (now, best for urgent and overnight) or "Casuals ask, you choose" (they tap "I can do this", you pick from a list sorted by reliability). Change G shows the list for both.

3. How is reliability worked out?

Code works it out from facts the app already logs, never AI. It's a number about people, so casuals should be able to see their own.

Recommended: over the last 90 days, shifts worked ÷ (shifts worked + no-shows + cancellations under 48 hours). Show the counts beside it. "Regular" means worked in 6 of the last 8 weeks. Shown to the office and to the casual on their Profile. Should it change who's offered first? Not yet: decide after a month of pilot data.

4. Which ways can an offer reach a casual?

The back end already tries the app notification first, then a text, then email. The screens and settings still say WhatsApp.

Recommended: app notification, then a text through the firm's Webex account, then email. A company WhatsApp (WhatsApp Business through Meta) only if the firm wants it later: it needs Meta's approval of each message and costs per message. Note: app notifications are off on the demo server; finishing them (Phase 4's open "Web push" task) comes first.

5. Alerts: who gets told, and how?

Today nothing tells a resourcer a cancellation has come in. The office gets an email only when a place is still free 3 hours before start (a firm setting).

Recommended: in the office, a live feed and a pop-up for each cancellation (on by default); a phone or desktop notification for cancellations under 48 hours and gaps still open 6 hours before start; each resourcer chooses, and only for their contracts.
