"Still coming?" in the day before a shift, before and after

What a casual sees on her pass and on her shift's page from 24 hours before a booked shift, and what the office sees on the dashboard's Coming up. Phones at their real 390px, light and dark; the laptop row is 1,280px wide at 150% scaling (an 853px window). Made-up firm and casual: Acme Events, Tess Comer. A dashed blue outline marks what 28.14 adds.

  • From 24 hours before the start until the start, the pass and the shift's page show "Still coming?" with "Yes, I'm coming" and "Can't make it?" (the cancel sheet, unchanged).
  • After the tap: "Thanks. The office can see you're coming.", then the card reads "You said you're coming (Fri 13:02)".
  • Online only: the copy kept for no signal shows no card at all.
  • The reminder pushes end "Tap to say you're coming."; the emails carry the same line; WhatsApp and texts are unchanged.
  • The office's Coming up: a green "Coming 13:02" or a grey "Not answered yet". Neither does anything by itself.

The pass on Home, the day before

Before, the pass had "Can't make it?" under Directions and Details. After, in the day before the shift, the card holds both answers. Directions stays the main button, so "Yes, I'm coming" is clear glass here.

The shift's page, opened from the reminder

After, the card sits right under the shift's card, with "Yes, I'm coming" as the page's main button. At the foot "Ask for a change" then stands alone, since "Can't make it?" is in the card.

The office: Coming up, on a laptop

Each person on a session starting within 24 hours: a green "Coming 13:02" (read as "Ann Udo said they're coming, at 13:02 on Fri 6 Nov.") or a grey "Not answered yet". Where the firm's own system sends the reminders, nothing shows unless she answered.

The reminder push

Before: "Tomorrow: Still Hall" / "08:00–16:00. Tap for instructions." After: "Tomorrow: Still Hall" / "08:00–16:00. Tap to say you're coming." (2 hours before: "Starts at 08:00. Tap to say you're coming."). Tapping opens the shift's page, where the card waits. The WhatsApp and text reminders keep their approved words.
