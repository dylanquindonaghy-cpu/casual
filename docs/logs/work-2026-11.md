### The guardrails review (fixes made)

No golden rule was broken. What it found, and what changed:

- **Busy days:** the "places still open" alert looked only at the 50 soonest sessions, so on a big
  day sessions after those were never reached. It now works through every session, 50 at a time.
- **The summary's figures** came from the page's list, which stops at 100. They're now counted on
  their own, from 08:00 yesterday (not "the last 24 hours", which was wrong when it went late).
- **Two alert jobs at once** could lock two people in opposite orders (Postgres would undo one and
  retry). Each batch now claims people in one order.
- **A subscription follows only the person who chose it.** Opening Updates, or the browser renewing
  a subscription, now changes only a subscription already that office user's. A casual's in the
  same browser is never taken on by itself.
- **Smaller fixes:** the log says "Sending … a notification" (it's written before it goes). A sixth
  browser dropping the oldest is logged. A taken-over browser counts as its new owner's newest. A
  request body is never read past 4 KB, even when its size isn't declared. "1 needs action".
- **Also found by the full test run:** the firm data export now leaves out office browsers' push
  keys, as it does casuals'.
- **New tests:** a disabled admin gets nothing, more sessions than one batch, two jobs at once, the
  summary's window, renewing, taking over, and the request body checks.
