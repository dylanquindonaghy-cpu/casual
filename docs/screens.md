# Screens

Mobile first for staff. Desktop first for the office (resourcers work on laptops), but usable
on a phone for 6am problems.

Every page on a firm's address, staff and office, opens with the firm's bar (Phase 8,
`src/app/brand-bar.tsx`): its logo (when it has one; decorative, the name is beside it) and its
name, on its brand colour with black or white text, whichever reads better. The platform's own
address has no bar.

**General states (every staff page)**
- Page not found (an old or mistyped link, or a firm address that doesn't exist;
  `src/app/not-found.tsx`): "We can't find that page." / "The link may be old or mistyped." /
  "Go to my shifts". Never "404".
- A page fails (`src/app/error.tsx`): "Something went wrong" / "It's not you. Try again in a
  moment." / "If it keeps happening, contact the office." / "Try again" / "Go to my shifts". With
  no network at all: "No signal" / "Check your signal and try again." "Try again" fetches the page
  from the server again (Next's `retry()`). It's disabled until the page is ready to respond to a
  tap, and while there's no signal (it comes back on, with "Something went wrong", when the signal
  does). "Go to my shifts" is a full page load, so it recovers on My shifts itself too.
- The whole page fails, frame and all (`src/app/global-error.tsx`): the same words, without "Go to
  my shifts". "Try again" loads the page again (as Next's own whole-page error does), even before
  the page's scripts have loaded.
- A tap fails (take, cancel, request, review, profile, pay code, login): "Something went wrong. Try
  again." With no network at all: "No signal. Try again when you have signal."
  (`tryAgainText()`, `src/lib/portal/copy.ts`).
- `tests/unit/copy.plain-language.test.ts` renders every staff screen in every state above and in
  the table. It fails on jargon (error, invalid, session, token, credential, eligible...), raw codes
  (`door_supervisor`), "undefined" or "null" in the text, and sentences over 25 words. A new staff
  screen or state goes in that test.

**Copy rules for staff screens**
- Short sentences. Plain words. No jargon ("roster plug", "tenant").
- Buttons say what happens: "Take shift", "Cancel this shift", "Send request".
- Every action ends with a confirmation the worker can screenshot: what, when, reference.
- All system messages signed "[Firm] Shift Team".
- Large tap targets, readable text sizes, high contrast (older workers use this too).
- "My shifts" is cached by a service worker so it opens with poor signal at venues (built in Phase 3: ADR-016).
