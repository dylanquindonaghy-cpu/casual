What's new: charts that show what needs work

This is a mock-up, not the app, on the demo's made-up data. Nothing in the office has changed yet. The look is the round 3 you approved; this version adds the charts.

  • Charts instead of lists. The dashboard's week strip and its three text panels (Needs attention, Waiting on others, Sorted today) are now six charts. Each answers one question: where are the gaps, which events need people, how offers are going, what has waited longest, who cancelled at short notice, and whose licence runs out soon.
  • Pick anything to see the rows. Every bar, dot and number opens a panel with the real items behind it and the button to act on each: Offer, Mark who worked, Check. On a phone the panel slides up from the bottom. The four counts at the top open their lists too.
  • Colour means the same everywhere. Red is urgent, amber needs you, blue is waiting on someone else, green is done, grey is fine, the same as the chips.
  • Numbers when you want them. Each chart has a Numbers button that swaps it for a table. Point at a bar, or tab to it, for its exact figures.
  • Built from what the office already records. Places per day, offers and their replies, pay queries and when they were sent, cancellations with their notice, and licence end dates. Nothing new has to be collected.

Checked for colour blindness

The chart colours passed a colour-blindness check in light and dark. Amber and grey are a little faint on white, so the bars also carry their numbers, and every chart has its table.
