In one line: Haulix looks expensive because it's restrained (one colour, flat surfaces) and because it shows the job itself (the map, the truck). ShiftDesk today has the opposite of both: lots of colour and glass, and no picture of its world.

Your decisions

1. Move away from glass?

You approved round 7 (Liquid Glass) on 26 Oct. Round 8 replaces its pastel wash and frosted white glass in the office with Haulix's charcoal, keeping translucent, sharp glass on the panels and cards (your ask on 2 Nov).

Recommended: yes for the office. The casual app keeps its look for now: it's used outdoors on phones, where light and clear works better.

2. Dark by default?

Haulix is dark only.

Recommended: dark by default for the office, with a light version built on the same flat rules for anyone who prefers it.

3. The accent: hi-vis yellow or the firm's own colour?

Haulix uses one pale lime. Hi-vis yellow does the same job and comes from event security itself.

Recommended: hi-vis in the office; the firm's colour stays on its mark and in the casual app.

4. The venue map needs a map service

Venues already have postcodes, and postcodes.io already turns them into coordinates. Drawing streets needs map tiles.

Recommended: MapLibre (open source) with a dark style from a UK or EU tile host, or a self-hosted map file. A new supplier, so your call; without it, the dashboard falls back to the cover grid by venue and day.
