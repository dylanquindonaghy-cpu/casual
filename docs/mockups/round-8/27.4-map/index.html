What changes from today

  • The map moves to what you pick: a pin, a session in the list, or a tab. Today it stays where it is and only the card changes.
  • Every session at a venue is one tap from its pin, with "1 of 2" and arrows on the card. Today a pin only opens the venue's first session.
  • "To offer" moves onto the map as the list button, so its column under the map goes. Updates and "Who worked?" stay under the map.
  • The map's buttons: the list, zoom in and out, and back to every venue. Today it has zoom in and out only.
  • The map is a little taller (600px on a laptop, was about 530) so the card's new row fits without scrolling.
  • On a phone the map is on top, the card under it, and the list under the card when it's open. Only the chosen pin shows its name, so names don't pile up.

Kept as today: the card's words and buttons, Filter and "Show filled venues too" (left off this mock-up), the key, and the plain list for keyboard and screen reader users. The card's own fixes (its bar and the repeated "Urgent") are in the 27.5 mock-up.
