What's different from round 1

This is a mock-up, not the app: only the dashboard is drawn, on the demo's made-up data. Nothing in the office has changed. Press things: every card, button and menu row answers.

  • Colour. The four cards are full colour now (coral, amber, blue, green): flat colour, no gradients, with a coloured shadow under each. The panels below carry the same colour, so "needs you", "waiting" and "sorted" read at a glance. The menu's counts take their card's colour too.
  • Layers. Four you can see: the window, the page sheet sitting on it, the cards and panels on the sheet, and the rows inside the panels. Each has a lit top edge and its own shadow, so it sits above the one under it.
  • Texture. A fine grain over the window and the coloured surfaces (like your HITE EQ reference), and grooves the bars sit in. No gradients anywhere.
  • Press feedback. Anything you press dips and springs back. The menu's highlight slides to what you pick. Offer shows it's sending, then a tick. The ··· menu grows out of its button. Tap a day in "This week" to see its numbers. Tap a card to jump to its section.
  • No scrollbar in the menu. Rows are 34px with a mouse (44px on touch), and the how-to guide has moved into your account menu (SA, top of the menu), so the whole menu fits a laptop window 640px tall. If a shorter window still can't hold it, it scrolls with no bar and fades at the edge instead.
  • Nothing moves on its own. Motion only answers you, and it switches off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Fonts

As option A: DM Sans for words, DM Mono for numbers, Newsreader semibold for the greeting.
