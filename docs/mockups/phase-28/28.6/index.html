ShiftDesk · Phase 28 · task 28.6

Message the office about a shift, before and after

What a casual sees on Messages when she comes from a shift (28.2's link, /messages?shift={id}&about={pay|late|started|other}), and what a resourcer sees on that message's page in Enquiries. Phones at their real 390px, light and dark; the laptop frames are 1,280px wide at 150% scaling (an 853px window). Made-up firm and people: Acme Events, Kay Morgan, a Steward shift on Sat 7 Nov at 10:30. A dashed blue outline marks what 28.6 adds or changes.

  • Opened from a shift she's booked on or can see, that hasn't ended: "About: Steward · Sat 7 Nov, 10:30" sits above the box, and the box holds a ready sentence she can change.
  • Sent: "Sent. The office will answer here." A message about a shift is never sorted by keyword or AI and gets no automatic answer: a person reads it.
  • If the shift ended before she tapped Send, nothing is kept: the message is sorted as any other and the page says "Sent. The answer comes here." A worked shift's pay question still gets the pay page's link (golden rule 9).
  • Messages' own words and look: a two-sentence intro, the box is "Your message", "No messages yet." sits in the empty box, the bubbles are 12px round.
  • In Enquiries the message waits in Need a person, and its page says "About: Steward, Sat 7 Nov 10:30" on her message's own bubble, linked to the session (a later message names its own session, or none).
  • Unchanged: 20 messages an hour at most, 5 automatic answers an hour at most, and the page looking again every few seconds for a minute after a send.

Messages, opened from a shift

Before, the link's shift was ignored: the page opened as always, with a 51-word intro and an empty box labelled "Write to the office". After, the shift is named and the box is ready to send. The three ready sentences are "What's the pay for this shift?", "I'm running late. I'll be there about …" and "This shift has started and I need to talk to someone."; about=other leaves the box empty under the About line.

The last frame is the page with no shift named: a shift that has ended, a draft or cancelled one, one she isn't booked on and can't see, another firm's, or a link the app doesn't make. It works as before, with the new words and look.

Sent

Her message shows in the thread at once. After 28.6, a message that kept its shift says "Sent. The office will answer here." and no automatic answer comes. The About line goes once it's sent, so her next message is about nothing in particular. The words after "Sent." come from what the send stored, so a shift that ended between opening the page and tapping Send gets today's words and today's answer.

The last two frames: the Steward shift ran 10:30 to 18:30. Kay opened Messages about its pay at 18:25 and tapped Send at 18:41, after it ended. The send keeps no shift, the message is sorted as any other, and a worked shift's pay question gets the pay page's link, towards a pay query for payroll.

The message in Enquiries

Before, "What's the pay for this shift?" matched the pay keyword and was answered with the pay page's link, and nobody in the office had to look. After, a message about a shift that hasn't ended waits in Need a person. Its page names the session at the top of "The conversation", linked to the session's page, and says why it went to a person. The same shows in the pane beside the list from 1,024px.

The phone frames are cut short after the parts 28.6 changes; the rest of the page ("Considered but not offered", the reply form, "Back to Enquiries") is as before. The action log's entry for the message names the session, so it also shows in that session's history.
